# DHCW Design System roadmap

This is the public roadmap for the DHCW Design System.

It gives us a simple way to share what we're working on now, what we'd like to look at next and what we're thinking about for the future.

The roadmap isn't a fixed delivery plan. We'll keep it updated as we learn more from the teams using the Design System and our priorities change.

## About the roadmap

We group work into three areas:

- **Now** – things we're actively working on
- **Next** – things we'd like to focus on next
- **Later** – things we think are important but need more exploration

The further away something is, the more likely it is to change.

## Updating the roadmap

The roadmap is maintained by the DHCW Design System team.

If our priorities change, we'll move, add or remove items so the roadmap reflects where we're focusing our time.
